# Banks in the Americas offering SMEs non-lending operational services: case files and reported results (as of Nov 2026)

Notes conventions:
- [REPORTED] = figure in an earnings call, filing, regulator report, or bank press release with a number. [CLAIM] = marketing/vendor claim. [3RD-PARTY] = analyst/press/survey estimate. [SNIPPET] = taken from a search-engine summary; the page itself was NOT opened (fetch failed or not attempted) -> verify before using in a final deck.
- Research stopped early because the shared web-search budget ran out (see Gaps). Canada (RBC/TD/BMO/Scotiabank), Banamex, Banco Azteca, Davivienda, Banco Pichincha, Popular Dominicano, BCI Pagos detail, Inter, Banco do Brasil, Santander Brasil "Empreendedor" were NOT researched in depth.
- Overall pattern in what was found: in Latin America the dominant "beyond-credit" offer by far is merchant acquiring / collections (POS, QR, payment links, tap-to-phone), usually bundled with a business account and same-day settlement into the bank. ERP/accounting/e-invoicing is mostly done through partnerships (Itaú–Omie, BTG–Senior/Conta Azul, BB–ContaAzul/NetSuite) with little disclosed result data. Hard, bank-reported evidence linking these services to deposits is scarce; the best example found is Interbank/Izipay (Peru).

## Q1. Chile: what did banks offer SMEs beyond credit and what results did they report? (Santander/Getnet, Banco de Chile/Banchile Pagos, Itaú/Klap, BancoEstado/Compraquí, BCI Pagos)

### Takeaway
Chile is the clearest LatAm natural experiment: after the four-party acquiring model began in 2020, Santander built Getnet Chile (launched ~March 2021) and in ~4 years took it to >200k merchants and roughly 19–30% of card-present acquiring (depending on source), eroding Transbank from 70–80% to 40–50%; Banco de Chile (Banchile Pagos, late 2025) and Itaú (bought Klap, Dec 2025) followed, i.e., the main banks now treat own acquiring as core to the SME franchise. No bank disclosed account-opening/deposit cross-sell figures from acquiring.

### Cited Findings

**Case: Santander Chile – Getnet Chile (built in-house with Santander group tech; launched ~March 2021)**
- What/how: bank-owned acquirer (POS, smart POS, mPOS) built with Santander's global Getnet platform; began operating in Chile in March 2021 (URL/press-release date) — [Santander press release title, 2021/03](https://www.santander.com/es/sala-de-comunicacion/notas-de-prensa/2021/03/comienza-a-operar-en-chile-getnet-la-nueva-red-de-adquirencia-de-banco-santander) [SNIPPET: title/URL only].
- Year 1 (dated 31 Mar 2022): >60,000 merchants, of which ~56,000 SMEs/entrepreneurs; >4,000 large companies; >120 million transactions for ~CLP 2.1 trillion (~USD 2.6bn) in 12 months; present in 97% of communes [REPORTED by company] — [El Dínamo, 31-Mar-2022](https://www.eldinamo.cl/pais/2022/03/31/a-un-ano-de-su-lanzamiento-getnet-llega-al-97-de-las-comunas-del-pais/).
- Bundle with account (Jan 2022): Santander launched a current account for small entrepreneurs ("Santander Prospera" per the SME manager's quote) bundled with a Getnet mPOS: plan 0.08 UF/month (~CLP 2,500, VAT incl.), mPOS device CLP 19,990, same-day settlement incl. weekends/holidays, unlimited free transfers, free web page with shopping cart, 100% digital onboarding, no minimum sales; stated goal "fomentar la bancarización de las PYME". No cross-sell metrics given — [Diario Financiero, 14-Jan-2022](https://www.df.cl/mercados/banca-fintech/banco-santander-sale-a-conquistar-a-las-pyme-junto-con-getnet-con-nuevo).
- Feb 2025: >200,000 associated merchants; sales credited same day into Santander accounts 365 days/year; added non-payment services on smart POS (transport-card top-ups, utility bill payments, prepaid mobile top-ups, cash or card) [REPORTED/CLAIM: "red de adquirencia de mayor crecimiento", no share given] — [Santander press release, 21-Feb-2025](https://www.santander.com/es/sala-de-comunicacion/notas-de-prensa/2025/02/getnet-lanza-en-chile-nuevas-funcionalidades-para-potenciar-a-los-comercios-a-traves-de-innovadoras-oportunidades-de-negocio).
- Nov 2025: >316,000 POS terminals operating; 18.9% share of transactions with physical cards [company figure] — [La Tercera, 19-Nov-2025](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/santander-chile-propone-incorporar-a-pagonxt-como-socio-estrategico-de-getnet/).
- Ownership change (announced 19-Nov-2025; EGM 10-Dec-2025): PagoNxt (Santander global payments) to take 49.99% of Getnet Chile, Santander Chile keeps 50.01% and control; cash payment of $41,600 million (CLP) plus a 7-year renewable services contract in favour of Banco Santander Chile valued at ~$45,200 million; rationale: scale economies, global innovation; PagoNxt 2024 globally: EUR 222bn volume, 1.2M merchants — [La Tercera, 19-Nov-2025](https://www.latercera.com/pulso/noticia/santander-chile-propone-incorporar-a-pagonxt-como-socio-estrategico-de-getnet/). A Fintool summary instead cites "CLP 68 billion upfront plus 10% of net operating revenues for 7+ years" [SNIPPET, conflicting — use La Tercera figures or verify in Santander Chile hecho esencial].
- Market share — three non-identical measures (do not mix):
  - FNE (competition authority) in its Klap/Itaú approval report (6-Jul-2026): card-present acquiring, Transbank 70–80% (2023) -> 40–50% (2025); Getnet 10–20% (2023) -> 20–30% (2025); Mercado Pago 0–5% -> 10–20%; card-not-present 2025: Transbank ~50%, Mercado Pago 20–30%, Kushki 20–30% [REPORTED, regulator ranges] — [BioBioChile, 7-Jul-2026](https://www.biobiochile.cl/noticias/economia/negocios-y-empresas/2026/07/07/transbank-ya-no-compite-solo-las-marcas-que-han-ganado-terreno-en-el-negocio-de-las-maquinas-de-pago.shtml).
  - FNE based on H1-2024 financial statements: Transbank 71.5% of card transactions; ChilePay/CNC 2025 merchant survey: Transbank 61.4%, Getnet 25.4%, Mercado Pago 10.6%, Compraquí (BancoEstado) 6.2%, BCI Pagos 4.8%, SumUp 4.3%, Klap 2.2% [3RD-PARTY survey] — [Emol, 1-Dec-2025](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/12/01/1184509/actores-desafian-reinado-transbank.html).
- No disclosure found of Getnet Chile revenue, profitability, % of Getnet merchants that are/became Santander account holders, or deposit impact. A search summary noted Getnet Chile financial statements (2020–2024) showing losses in some periods [SNIPPET, not verified] — [Santander Chile IR static files](https://santandercl.gcs-web.com/static-files/7fae3d5f-9e5a-4632-92ce-e5ef25f0b8a7).

- Compraquí: 6.2% merchant-survey share in 2025 (ChilePay/CNC) — [Emol, 1-Dec-2025](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/12/01/1184509/actores-desafian-reinado-transbank.html).

**Market context**: four-party model began early 2020; 72% of household consumption made by card (Banco Central); merchants accepting cards grew almost 6x in ten years; CMF registers 10 card acquirers — [Emol, 1-Dec-2025](https://www.emol.com/noticias/Economia/2025/12/01/1184509/actores-desafian-reinado-transbank.html).

### Inferences
- Getnet's trajectory (~60k merchants after 1 year -> >200k after 4 years; 0 -> 20–30% card-present share by 2025) shows a bank-owned acquirer can win share quickly when the market opens, mainly with same-day settlement into the bank's own account + low-cost bundles. The 2025 move to sell 49.99% to PagoNxt suggests Santander Chile values scale/technology from the group over full local ownership — not evidence of failure, but also no proof of standalone profitability.
- Banchile Pagos' slow start (~6,800 merchants after ~6 months vs 150k target) and early GM turnover is a caution for late entrants; Itaú chose to buy (Klap) rather than build.
- Mercado Pago's jump (0–5% -> 10–20%) shows non-bank wallets, not just banks, captured share from Transbank.

### Gaps
- No Chilean bank disclosed: revenue/profit of its acquirer, % of acquiring merchants that opened accounts, deposit balances attributable to settlement, NPS or churn. The Santander Chile 20-F / memoria 2025 should be checked for Getnet P&L and SME client growth.
- Banco de Chile/BancoEstado non-financial SME programs (e-invoicing, training "Pyme" programs) not researched. No evidence found of a Banco de Chile–Nubox alliance (Nubox was originally Servipyme, a Banco del Desarrollo subsidiary, per [Nubox blog](https://blog.nubox.com/empresas/nubox-apunta-a-un-millon-de-emprendedores) [SNIPPET]).
- BCI's own acquisitions, MACH business features, Scotiabank Chile not covered.

## Q2. Peru: Interbank (Izipay, Plin) and BCP/Credicorp (Yape, Culqi) — what was offered and what were the results?

### Takeaway
Interbank/IFS gives the single best piece of LatAm evidence that an owned acquirer drives SME deposits: management said Izipay flows grew 60% and "supported a 26% deposit growth" (FY2025, Feb-2026 call). Credicorp's Yape has massive scale (16.4M MAU, Mar-2026) and is now a material revenue line, but Credicorp discloses little about merchant/SME tools or Culqi.

### Cited Findings

**Case: Interbank / Intercorp Financial Services – Izipay (acquired 2022) + Plin (wallet)**
- IFS acquired Izipay (Aug 2022 article); merchants affiliated +53% YoY; acquiring commissions = 89% of Izipay revenue; CFO Michela Casassa: Izipay "se usará como una red de distribución de productos de Interbank" — [Gestión, 18-Aug-2022](https://gestion.pe/economia/empresas/izipay-tambien-sera-usado-como-red-para-distribuir-productos-de-interbank-noticia/).
- FY2025 earnings call (12-Feb-2026) [REPORTED, management statements]: Izipay flows +60% over the past year, driven by small businesses; Izipay flows "supported a 26% deposit growth"; deposit share figures garbled in transcript ("11% of wholesale deposits or 33% of wholesale low-cost deposits"); Izipay and bank commercial teams collaborating on integrated solutions; management expects Izipay-linked funds to keep flowing into bank accounts (low-cost funding). No Izipay revenue/profit disclosed. Small-business loans +25% YoY, small business disbursements +60%; efficient commercial deposits +15.5%; commercial digital adoption 74%; commercial NPS 68 — [IFS FY2025 earnings call transcript, Motley Fool, 12-Feb-2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/intercorp-financial-ifs-earnings-call-transcript/).
- Plin (same call): 2.6M MAU; transactions +48%; P2M = 60% of transactions; est. ~15% of P2P/P2M market (management says "no official source"); 2025 launches Plin Corredores, Plin WhatsApp, Plin eCommerce; CEO: Plin users are more primary-bank customers with higher NPS and lower churn — no figures given [CLAIM without numbers] — [Motley Fool transcript, 12-Feb-2026](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/02/12/intercorp-financial-ifs-earnings-call-transcript/).
- IFS net income S/580m in 2Q25 (2x YoY), management cited growing Interbank–Izipay synergies [SNIPPET] — [Infomercado](https://infomercado.pe/ifs-registro-s-580-millones-de-utilidad-neta-en-el-segundo-trimestre-de-2025/).

**Case: BCP / Credicorp – Yape (built in-house, 2016 consumer wallet, now with business features) and Culqi (invested/acquired via Krealo, Dec 2018)**
- Culqi: Krealo (Credicorp's digital venture unit) signed a strategic alliance/investment with Culqi (online payments gateway) on 21-Dec-2018; at the time Culqi had 3,500 clients processing S/22m/month; aim: work with BCP and Mibanco on SME solutions — [Peru Retail, 21-Dec-2018](https://www.peru-retail.com/peru-credicorp-potenciara-bcp-mibanco-alianza-culqi/).
- Culqi new CEO Spencer Friedman from 1-Sep-2025; "Culqi Connect" processing highway with Yape as first entity integrated [SNIPPET, undated] — [LatamFintech](https://www.latamfintech.co/articles/fintech-culqi-desarrolla-culqi-connect-para-facilitar-la-conexion-de-comercios-con-tapp-la-nueva-infraestructura-de-pagos-de-peru).
- Yape 2Q25: ~15M MAU (~75% of economically active population); 54.5 transactions per MAU/month; >1.9M clients accessed credit in the quarter; innovation portfolio = 6.2% of risk-adjusted revenue (target 10% in 2026); growth driven by retail and SME lending plus marketplace/commerce; expected to become Credicorp's 2nd-largest business line in 3–5 years — [Peru Retail, 20-Aug-2025](https://www.peru-retail.com/credicorp-reporta-utilidad-neta-de-s-1822-millones-en-el-2t25-y-proyecta-mayor-crecimiento-de-yape/).
- Yape 4Q25: 15.9M MAU; 7.2% of Credicorp risk-adjusted revenue [SNIPPET from Credicorp 4Q25 release; fetch blocked] — [Credicorp IR](https://credicorp.gcs-web.com/node/19236/html).
- Yape 1Q26 (to Mar-2026): 16.4M MAU (82% of EAP); financial business = 50% of Yape revenue (mainly loans); payments & QR = 47% of revenue; innovation businesses = 9% of risk-adjusted revenue (target 10% by end-2026); Credicorp ROE 21.1% — [Peru Retail, 18-May-2026](https://www.peru-retail.com/credicorp-utilidad-neta-aumenta-16-1-en-el-1t26-respaldada-por-los-negocios-digitales/).
- Yape Bolivia: >3.7M registered users, 1.7M businesses operating with the app (Dec 2025) — [Infobae, 10-Dec-2025](https://www.infobae.com/peru/2025/12/11/yape-tambien-domina-en-bolivia-se-ha-consolidado-como-la-billetera-digital-lider-y-ahora-apunta-a-llegar-al-50-de-su-poblacion/).
- Peru merchant acceptance context: Capece estimates 2.1M merchants accept digital payments, >1.1M still collect only in person [SNIPPET] — [Gestión](https://gestion.pe/tu-dinero/yape-ya-se-codea-con-la-elite-de-los-bancos-peruanos-en-que-dio-el-gran-salto-noticia/).

### Inferences
- Peru shows two models: Interbank bought a merchant acquirer and explicitly uses it as a deposit/distribution funnel (quantified link to deposit growth); BCP grew a mass wallet whose merchant side (QR P2M) is monetized mainly through lending (50% of Yape revenue) rather than SME software.
- Yape's revenue split (47% payments/QR) indicates merchant/payments fees can be a real revenue line at scale, not just a loss leader.

### Gaps
- Number of Yape business/merchant users in Peru, Yape Empresas features/pricing, Culqi TPV/merchant counts and profitability — not found in opened sources. A circulating "1.7 million affiliated merchants" figure appears to be Yape Bolivia; do not attribute to Peru without verification.
- BBVA Perú, Scotiabank Perú, Niubiz (BCP/Scotiabank-owned acquirer) not covered.

## Q3. Colombia: Bancolombia / Grupo Cibest (Wompi, Nequi Negocios) and others

### Takeaway
Grupo Cibest (Bancolombia's holding since 2025) built Wompi (payments gateway/acquiring/QR) and Nequi Negocios; Wompi reported ~COP 50 trillion processed in 2025, ~40k monthly active merchants, +150% YoY (secondary source). ## Q4. Brazil: Itaú (Rede, Omie ERP), Bradesco+BB (Cielo), Santander (Getnet), cooperatives (Sicoob/Sipag), BTG

### Takeaway
Brazil is where the "integrated bank + acquirer" thesis has the strongest market evidence: Itaú re-integrated Rede (2012 delisting) and Rede overtook Cielo; Bradesco and BB then delisted Cielo (2024) explicitly to integrate acquiring with banking; by Jan-2026 Rede led by volume (18%) and gained +5pp in user share, while Santander's Getnet lost share. Bank-ERP partnerships (Itaú–Omie, BTG–Senior) exist but report only vendor productivity claims.

### Cited Findings

**Case: Itaú Unibanco – Rede (acquirer, re-integrated 2012) + "Itaú Meu Negócio gestão by Omie" (ERP partnership, 2021)**
- Jan-2026 acquiring share (UBS BB data): by users Rede 25% (+5pp YoY), Cielo 28% (+2pp), PagBank 26% (+2pp), Stone 22% (−2pp), Getnet 9% (−3pp); by volume Rede 18% (sole leader), Cielo 17%, PagBank 17%, Stone 15% (−2pp), Getnet 5% (−2pp); standalone digital accounts fell from 46% to 40% of the market; UBS BB: "os bancos estão fortalecendo seu papel como canal de distribuição fundamental"; Itaú sells Rede within a bundle of account + credit + financial management [3RD-PARTY] — [Let's Money, 21-Mar-2026 (upd. 12-Sep-2026)](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/noticias/rede-itau-lidera-volume-adquirentes/).
- Itaú 2025: acquiring card volume reached R$1 trillion (+22.8%) [SNIPPET] — [Let's Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/noticias/itau-lucro-recorde-46bi/).
- Illustration of the integrated model by ex-Redecard CFO Edson Santos: of every R$100 of card transactions, ~R$25 went through Rede, ~R$35 through Itaú cards and ~R$25 into Itaú accounts; Itaú's 2012 Rede tender "worked well"; Rede overtook Cielo in 2023 [3RD-PARTY opinion] — [Brazil Journal, 5-Feb-2024](https://braziljournal.com/bradesco-e-bb-lancam-opa-pela-cielo-e-uma-aposta-no-modelo-integrado).
- Rede's zero-cost receivables prepayment for merchants receiving in Itaú accounts had paid >R$2bn to merchants (2019); CADE investigation into settlement terms closed without sanction in Dec-2025 [SNIPPET] — [Seu Dinheiro, 2019](https://www.seudinheiro.com/2019/empresas/rede-do-itau-paga-mais-de-r-2-bilhoes-a-lojistas-com-politica-de-custo-zero-de-antecipacao/); [Mercado&Consumo, 11-Dec-2025](https://mercadoeconsumo.com.br/11/12/2025/economia/itau-e-redecard-escapam-de-sancao-do-cade-em-investigacao-sobre-prazos-de-liquidacao/).
- ERP: "Itaú Meu Negócio gestão by Omie" (partnership announced 2021): cloud ERP contracted via Itaú app or RM, with Pix QR/BoleCode collections inside the ERP; Omie-run survey (Apr-2023): +80% administrative productivity, +20% financial productivity, ~50% better performance than other Omie clients [CLAIM, vendor survey]; no client numbers or pricing disclosed — [Omie blog, Apr-2023 (upd. 31-Jan-2024)](https://www.omie.com.br/blog/itau-unibanco-ajuda-clientes-pj-com-solucao-de-gestao-empresarial/index.md).
- 2025: Itaú launched "Itaú Emps", an AI-driven digital PJ account for entrepreneurs [SNIPPET] — [MobileTime, 21-Jul-2025](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/21/07/2025/itau-pj-ia-itau-emps/).
