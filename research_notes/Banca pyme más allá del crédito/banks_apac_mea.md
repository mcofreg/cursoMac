# Banks in Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Africa offering SMEs non-lending operational services: case files and reported results (as of Nov 2026)

Method note for the report writer: research done 10 Nov 2026. The shared web-search budget ran out partway through, so the cases below are the ones I could check against pages I actually opened. Labels used:
- **[Reported]**: figure from a bank results release, annual report or bank press release (unaudited unless stated).
- **[Award write-up]**: figure from Euromoney or Global Finance award text, which is based on bank submissions. Use with care.
- **[Marketing]**: claim from a product page, advertorial or vendor press release.
- **[Snippet-only, not opened]**: figure seen only in a search-result summary, because the page could not be opened (403/401 or a blank render). Do not cite it as verified.

Coverage: 14 cases have usable evidence. DBS, UOB, Wio, Mashreq, Standard Bank, FNB, BRI, HDFC, CBA and SC/Solv have the best data. Many candidates in the brief remain unverified; they are listed under Gaps.

## Q1. Singapore / Southeast Asia: DBS, UOB, BRI, Kasikornbank: what was offered, how, and with what results?

### Takeaway
DBS has the region's only published comparison of digital and traditional customer economics. Digital customers earned a 27% ROE against 19% for traditional ones, but the figure is from 2017 and mixes consumer and SME customers, with no SME-only split. UOB's BizSmart (2016) is the clearest bank-curated SaaS bundle. Its only outcome metric is that almost a third of the Malaysian SMEs that adopted UOB SmartBusiness made UOB their main bank. BRI's free LinkUMKM is the largest by reach (13.6m MSME users in Sep 2025), but BRI reports usage only, not financial outcomes.

### Cited Findings

**Case 1: DBS Bank (Singapore). Built in-house: community platform, digital SME banking, AI assistant**
- What, how and when: DBS BusinessClass is an SME community and advisory app built by the bank. It offers private and group discussions with peers and investors, in-app chat with BusinessClass advisors, curated news and offline roundtables and seminars. It was already live in Singapore when it launched in Hong Kong on 13 July 2016, with China, India, Indonesia and Taiwan planned next. The release reports no results. [Reported] [DBS HK press release, 13 Jul 2016](https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_Hong_Kong_Rolls_Out_DBS_BusinessClass_Programme_To_Support_SME_Community)
- Membership: a June 2023 DBS Hong Kong release reportedly describes BusinessClass as a networking platform for "250,000 members across Asia". An older 2016 figure was 15,000 members in six countries. [Snippet-only, not opened]. Some benefits, such as deals, are limited to DBS SME Banking customers. [Snippet-only, from dbs.com.sg BusinessClass pages]
- Digital vs traditional economics (2017, consumer and SME combined): CFO Chng Sok Hui said "digital consumer and small and medium-sized enterprise customers are delivering 27% return on equity" against 19% for traditional customers. Digital customers grew at a 23% CAGR since 2015, while traditional customers shrank by 2% a year. [Award write-up / interview] [Euromoney, "DBS top of the digital class", 20 Dec 2017](https://www.euromoney.com/article/b163mv0dk41x45/dbs-top-of-the-digital-class)
- 2017 data for Singapore and Hong Kong: of about 6m customers, 2.5m (about 42%) were digital, and they produced 63% of profit. Revenue per digital customer was S$1,300 against S$600 for a traditional customer. [Reported via press] [Jakarta Post, 12 Jul 2018](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2018/07/12/dbs-starts-to-enjoy-returns-from-digitalization.html)
  - Conflict: a search snippet from DBS's 2017 annual report gave 27% vs **18%** ROE, and quoted a 34% vs 54% cost-income ratio. I could not open the PDF, which rendered blank, so this is unverified. [DBS AR17 pp. 22–23](https://www.dbs.com/annualreports/2017/pdfs/business-model/DBS-AR17-22-23.pdf)
- 2025: "over 20,000 unique corporate and SME customers have used DBS Joy", a GenAI corporate-banking assistant launched in July 2025, which DBS links to a 23% rise in customer-satisfaction scores. Other SME tools include SecureFX (lock FX rates up to one month ahead, up to USD 1m) and the ESG Ready Programme with Enterprise Singapore. [Reported] [DBS 2025 Annual Report via MarketScreener, 8 Mar 2026](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/dbs-2025-annual-report-ce7e5fd9da8af723)
- No SME-only figure for digital vs traditional income, ROE or retention was found for 2019–2026.

**Case 2: UOB (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand). Partnered: BizSmart / SmartBusiness bundle of third-party SaaS**
- What and how: BizSmart is a curated bundle of partner cloud tools covering accounting, HR and payroll, POS, payments, digital transactions and e-commerce. UOB integrates the tools with its accounts and does not build them. It launched in UOB Singapore in August 2016 and expanded to Malaysia in 2018 as "UOB SmartBusiness". [Reported] [The Asian Banker, Jan 2024](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/uob-smartbusiness-propels-sme-towards-digital-efficiency-and-growth)
- **Outcome metric:** "almost a third of SMEs that adopted UOB SmartBusiness switched to UOB Malaysia as their main banking partner". This is the only primacy and conversion result I found in the region. The article gives no user counts, revenue, deposits or pricing. [Reported via trade press] [The Asian Banker, Jan 2024](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/uob-smartbusiness-propels-sme-towards-digital-efficiency-and-growth)
- Xero partnership: Xero has been available in BizSmart since August 2016, with direct bank feeds into UOB business accounts. In July 2021, SMEs that qualified for the UOB "Start Digital" pack got six months of Xero free, so the pricing model is a subsidised trial followed by the partner's standard subscription. UOB said it "has helped more than 10,000 SMEs digitalise". [Marketing / partner release] [Xero media release, 26 Jul 2021](https://www.xero.com/us/media-releases/xero-teams-up-with-uob)
- UOB pages list other partners such as Financio, AutoCount, QuickBooks Online, Enterpryze and GutsyHQ (Singapore), and PEAK accounting (Thailand). [Snippet-only, from uob.com.sg and uobgroup.com pages]

**Case 3: Bank Rakyat Indonesia (BRI). Built: LinkUMKM, a free capability, community and digital-scoring platform for MSMEs**
- What: LinkUMKM offers more than 690 online training modules, a self-assessment ("Self-Assessment Naik Kelas" / "UMKM Smart") that scores businesses as traditional, developing or modern and recommends training, plus a community, a digital storefront ("Etalase Digital"), Rumah BUMN, UMKM Media and NIB business-ID registration. [Reported via press] [CNBC Indonesia, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/entrepreneur/20251104135740-25-682159/top-linkumkm-bri-catat-136-juta-pengguna-hingga-september-2025)
- Users: more than 13.6m MSME users at end-September 2025. [Reported] [CNBC Indonesia, 4 Nov 2025](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/entrepreneur/20251104135740-25-682159/top-linkumkm-bri-catat-136-juta-pengguna-hingga-september-2025)
- Trajectory [snippet-only, not opened]:
  - 12.9m users by August 2025, of which more than 9.9m had used digital scoring by end-June 2025 (Bisnis.com, 9 Aug 2025; returned 403).
  - 14.98m by end-2025 (BCA Sekuritas item).
  - About 16.46m by April 2026 (Industry.co.id URL slug; page returned 404).
- No figures were found linking LinkUMKM use to credit, deposits or revenue. The article does not mention a credit link.

**Case 4: Kasikornbank (Thailand). Built: K SHOP / K PLUS SHOP merchant QR-acceptance apps**
- K PLUS SHOP is opened from the consumer K PLUS app (one shop per K PLUS account, approved instantly). K SHOP accepts Alipay+ and WeChat Pay. K PLUS had 24.2m users at end-2025. [Snippet-only, not opened: Antom guide; kasikornbank.com page rendered empty](https://www.kasikornbank.com/en/personal/digital-banking/pages/k-plus-shop.aspx)
- I found no merchant count or results for K SHOP or K PLUS SHOP.

### Inferences
- DBS's widely quoted "digital customers are about 2x more valuable" evidence is from 2017, mixes consumer and SME customers, and is confounded by selection: the more active and larger customers adopt digital channels first. It supports correlation, not causation, for SME tools.
- UOB's "a third switched main bank" is the strongest primacy signal in Asia. There is no baseline (the switch rate among non-adopters), no denominator and no date, so it reads as a directional marketing-grade result.
- The UOB model (curate partner SaaS, subsidise the first months, add bank feeds) is low-capex. It appears to have lasted about 10 years (2016 to at least 2024), which suggests it was not abandoned.
- BRI's numbers are reach and registration metrics. BRI positions LinkUMKM as part of moving SMEs "up a class" (naik kelas) into its micro-lending funnel, but discloses no link to credit outcomes.

### Gaps
- No verified data for OCBC (Business app / Business Growth tools), Maybank, CIMB, SCB, DBS MAX, DBS's Xero integration or DBS "Business Digital". The search budget ran out before they could be checked.
- I could not verify the 250,000 BusinessClass member count or any DBS SME-only digital economics for 2019–2026.
- Kasikornbank merchant counts are not disclosed in anything I accessed.

## Q2. India: HDFC SmartHub Vyapar, ICICI, Kotak, Axis, and government rails

### Takeaway
HDFC Bank's SmartHub Vyapar is a white-labelled merchant super-app built on Mintoak's platform. HDFC claimed it was adding more than 75,000 merchants a month at launch (Oct 2022), and in 2025 it added a storefront and QR layer via Vyaparify. Neither HDFC nor Mintoak has published deposit, CASA, cross-sell or credit results; "activation rates, payment throughput and engagement increased" was stated without figures.

### Cited Findings

**Case 5: HDFC Bank SmartHub Vyapar. Partnered / white-label (Mintoak PaaS), launched October 2022**
- Features:
  - Interoperable acceptance of cards, UPI and QR, with voice alerts.
  - Payment links by SMS or email for remote collections.
  - Real-time view of settlements.
  - Payments to distributors and vendors, utility bills and GST.
  - A tool to broadcast offers on social media.
  - Fixed deposits opened in the app.
  - Pre-approved loans and credit cards.
- Claim: "more than 75,000 merchants every month". [Marketing / bank claim] [KNN India, 8 Oct 2022](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/msme/hdfc-bank-boasts-to-onboard-75000-sme-merchants-every-month-under-smarthub-vyapar-app)
- Build model: Mintoak's API-first, cloud-native platform is white-labelled as SmartHub Vyapar and described as PaaS integrated with the bank's systems. Mintoak's own platform grew "from less than 1,000 merchants to over a million" in two years. "Activation rates, total payment throughput, and merchant engagement score" increased, but no numbers are given. [Marketing / vendor PR] [PR Newswire (Mintoak), 5 Dec 2022](https://prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/mintoak-develops-merchant-solutions-to-fuel-business-growth-for-msmes-861588478.html)
- Extension: "My Business QR" with Vyaparify (9 Oct 2025). Registering through SmartHub Vyapar automatically creates an SEO-ready, WhatsApp-enabled digital storefront and catalogue, with Scan & Pay and Chat & Order through a WhatsApp chatbot. The release is an advertorial and gives no pricing or merchant figures. [Marketing] [Tribune India (VMPL advertorial), 9 Oct 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/hdfc-bank-launches-countrys-first-my-business-qr-powered-by-vyaparify-giving-bharats-smes-their-first-commerce-identity/amp)

**Case 6: ICICI Bank InstaBIZ (business banking app)**
- I found no InstaBIZ user or value metrics in what I could access. The Sep 2025 investor presentation PDF returned 403. ICICI reports more than 30m users for iMobile Pay, its retail app. [Snippet-only]

### Inferences
- In India, partnering with a merchant SaaS vendor (Mintoak) let HDFC scale distribution quickly. Without disclosed CASA or credit outcomes, the business case is not proven publicly.

### Gaps
- No verified data on ICICI InstaBIZ or Merchant Stack, Kotak Biz, Axis, IDFC First, or results linked to GST e-invoicing, OCEN or Account Aggregator cash-flow lending. These were not reached before the search budget ran out.

## Q3. North Asia / Japan / Korea / China

### Takeaway
The only verified item is structural. SMBC and Money Forward (a cloud accounting and back-office SaaS company) agreed a 50/50 digital-bank joint venture in July 2025. No SME results are published yet.

### Cited Findings

**Case 7: SMBC × Money Forward. Joint venture (bank and accounting SaaS)**
- On 22 July 2025, a new subsidiary, SMF Inc. (capital ¥250m), was set to convert in August 2025 into "SMBC Money Forward Bank Preparatory Corporation", owned 50/50 through a third-party share allotment. The article gives no product or launch date. [Reported via filings aggregator] [FilingReader, 22 Jul 2025](https://filingreader.com/news-wire/tokyo/2025-07-22/money-forward-and-smbc-finalize-digital-banking-joint-venture)
- Snippet-only, not opened:
  - The venture aims to improve financial services for Japanese SMEs with API-driven services.
  - Capital figures conflict across sources: ¥3bn capital plus ¥3bn reserve, versus SMBC investing ¥2.75bn.
  - Money Forward Cloud covers accounting, expenses and payroll.
- An SMBC PDF dated around 1 Dec 2025 links to Money Forward consumer ("HOME") campaign pages and appears to be a consumer tie-up, not SME. [SMBC news PDF](https://www.smbc.co.jp/news/pdf/j20251201_01.pdf). The text was unreadable, so treat this as an inference.

### Inferences
- In Japan, banks are moving toward co-owning accounting-SaaS-led banks rather than reselling software. This is the opposite direction from UOB's curated-marketplace model.

### Gaps
- No verified data for MUFG or Mizuho stakes or partnerships with freee or Money Forward, KB / Shinhan SOL Biz, Ping An / OneConnect or WeBank SME services.

## Q4. Australia / New Zealand: CBA, NAB, Westpac, ANZ, BNZ, ASB

### Takeaway
Commonwealth Bank is the verified case. It links non-lending SME benefits (a business loyalty programme with partner discounts, Smart terminal apps and pharmacy payments) explicitly to primacy: benefit tiers depend on transaction volumes and lending relationship. CBA has published reach (more than 340,000 eligible) but no outcomes.

### Cited Findings

**Case 8: Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA). Built plus partnered: Yello for Business, Smart terminal ecosystem, sector payment apps**
- Announced 17 June 2024:
  - Yello for Business, an extension of the retail CommBank Yello programme (launched 2023), which gives about A$400m of value a year to retail customers.
  - The Flexi Business Investment Account, a term deposit allowing 20% withdrawal without penalty.
  - CommBank Smart Health for Pharmacies, built with Scrypt Ventures after an 18-month pilot at 22 pharmacies.

  [Reported] [CBA media release, 17 Jun 2024](https://commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2024/06/cba-reimagines-business-banking.html)
- Yello for Business launched 18 March 2025 to more than 340,000 eligible small-business customers: sole traders and single-director companies holding a CBA business transaction account. Customers receive **one of two benefit sets based on transaction volumes and lending relationship**, reassessed monthly. Partners include nbn and mobile providers, Nine Ad Manager, Samsung (15% off), BioPak (20%), Kennards Hire (20%), Doshii (three months free, then 10% off) and workwear brands. No uptake or outcome data. [Reported] [CBA media release, 18 Mar 2025](https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2025/03/yello-for-business-expansion.html)
- Smart terminal: it succeeded the Albert tablet. Its App Marketplace is available on the Smart terminal only, and eligible merchants get Doshii free if they sign up before 31 Dec 2026. Card pricing moves from 1.1% to 0.99% from 1 Oct 2026. [Snippet-only, from commbank.com.au merchant pages and Inside Retail]

### Inferences
- CBA's design is notable: non-lending perks are rationed by primacy (transaction volumes and lending), so the programme is a retention and primacy lever, not a revenue line.

### Gaps
- No verified data for NAB (Xero/MYOB integrations), Westpac, ANZ (ANZ Plus business, ANZ Worldline merchant JV), BNZ or ASB.
- No CBA disclosure of Smart terminal merchant numbers, App Marketplace usage or Yello for Business uptake.
- NAB's 86 400 acquisition (about A$220m, 2021) was a retail neobank folded into UBank, so it is **not** an SME case. [Snippet-only: iTnews and NAB release]

## Q5. Middle East: Wio, Mashreq NEO BIZ / Mashreq Biz, Emirates NBD (E20)

### Takeaway
Wio Bank (UAE) is the strongest proof of economics in the whole sample. It is a digital SME-first bank with accounting and tax integrations, about 120,000 business entities and an NPS of 75 for Wio Business, and it reported AED622m net profit in 2025 after being profitable in its first full year (2023). Mashreq's NEOBIZ (2019) reached about 70,000 customers (combined with Business Banking) by Feb 2025, was migrated into "Mashreq Biz", and was exported to Pakistan in Oct 2026 with bookkeeping, payroll and invoicing on the roadmap. Emirates NBD's E20 digital business bank (2019) reports account-opening and revenue growth, not tool-level outcomes.

### Cited Findings

**Case 9: Wio Bank (UAE). Built (greenfield digital bank on Mambu core); SME-first**
- 2025 results:
  - Revenue AED1.24bn (+55%); net profit AED622m (+57%).
  - Assets AED61bn (+64%); customer deposits above AED57bn (+66%).
  - Wio Business served 120,000 entities; Wio Personal had more than 270,000 individuals.
  - NPS 75 for Wio Business and 76 for Wio Personal.
  - Products include "integrations with accounting and taxation systems", supply-chain finance (more than AED1bn disbursed), POS lending and SME credit cards.
  - About 25% of individual customers moved their salary to Wio's payroll platform.

  [Reported] [Entrepreneur Middle East, 18 Mar 2026](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/wio-bank-reports-aed-1-24-billion-revenue-up-55-in-2025)
- End-2024: more than 90,000 SME customers, about 5,000 new business accounts a month, onboarding in under 10 minutes and deposits and revenues quadrupled over two years. Ranked in the top 10 UAE banks by retail and SME deposits. [Award write-up] [Euromoney, 21 May 2025](https://www.euromoney.com/article/6w5lxvo0a9c8cs0k0k4k4ss8k/awards/awards-for-excellence/middle-easts-best-digital-bank-2025-wio-bank)
- Profitable in its first full year of operations (2023). The first Wio Business account opened 14 months after the build started, before retail. [Marketing / vendor] [Mambu, 18 Jun 2025](https://mambu.com/en/insights/articles/wio-bank-cpo-building-profit-mambu)
- 2026 SME award: Euromoney highlights banking linked to licensing and accounting workflows, and credit widened using data from day-to-day commercial activity. The figures are paywalled. [Award write-up] [Euromoney, 17 Jul 2026](https://www.euromoney.com/article/xn2kg4wzv5wkwc44os40owkg/corporate-banking/middle-easts-best-digital-bank-for-smes-2026-wio-bank/)

**Case 10: Mashreq (UAE, Pakistan). Built: NEOBIZ (2019), migrated into Mashreq Biz (2025), exported to Pakistan (2026)**
- On 24 Feb 2025, Mashreq Biz launched with a unified dashboard, instant FX accounts, bulk payments, deposit bookings, workflow approvals, multi-user access and "value-added services". Mashreq was migrating "over 70,000 existing NEOBIZ and Business Banking customers". NEOBIZ (2019) was "the first digital banking proposition in the UAE specifically designed for SMEs". [Reported] [Mashreq press release, 24 Feb 2025](https://www.mashreq.com/en/uae/news/2025/february/mashreq-launches-mashreq-biz/)
- Euromoney 2026 reportedly says the platform "has onboarded more than 85,000 customers", giving Mashreq a share of UAE SME customers above 20%. [Snippet-only, not opened (401)] [Euromoney, 2026](https://wporigin.euromoney.com/article/vu6vmwo85bks484sswk8css4/corporate-banking/middle-easts-best-bank-for-corporates-2026-mashreq/)
- Pakistan, 23 Oct 2026: NEO BIZ launched with digital account opening. Payroll, invoicing and invoice-based payments, business and virtual cards, transaction analytics and **bookkeeping** are planned. Supply-chain, tax and cash-flow-based financing will come later via BaaS partnerships. [Reported via trade press] [The Paypers, 23 Oct 2026](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/mashreq-launches-neo-biz-digital-banking-for-smes-in-pakistan)

**Case 11: Emirates NBD (UAE). Built: E20 digital business bank (Sep 2019), businessONLINE, smartTRADE**
- E20 gives new SMEs a business account through its app within 10 minutes. Over the prior year (published May 2024):
  - More than 7,000 new business accounts opened for new SMEs and startups.
  - SME lending up 43%.
  - SME banking revenue up 88%.
  - Net profit up 105% vs 2022.

  Non-lending services: businessONLINE (dashboards, remittances, cash management, APIs, host-to-host) and the smartTRADE portal. [Award write-up] [Euromoney, 23 May 2024](https://euromoney.com/article/2d7b7i4ope326bzsx0irk/awards/awards-for-excellence/middle-easts-best-bank-for-smes-emirates-nbd)
- A 2023 executive quote said "one in four UAE SMEs" bank with Emirates NBD. [Snippet-only, not opened (FF News 404)]

### Inferences
- Wio indicates that an SME-first, integrated operating stack (accounts, accounting and tax integrations, cards, data-led credit) can be profitable quickly in a market with high SME formation and corporate-tax and e-invoicing tailwinds. The profit is not attributable to the non-lending tools specifically.
- Mashreq's sequence (standalone digital SME brand, then folding into the core business platform, then exporting the brand) suggests the brand was absorbed rather than failed.

### Gaps
- No verified data for FAB, ADCB, Al Rajhi, SNB, QNB or Liv.
- Wio's shareholder structure was not verified in the sources I opened.
- No E20-only customer count.

## Q6. Africa: FNB, Standard Bank, Nedbank (plus Absa, Capitec, Equity, Ecobank and KCB, which are unverified)

### Takeaway
South Africa has the most mature "free operational tools" offer. FNB bundles Instant Accounting and Instant Payroll free for business-account clients, but has never published adoption figures. Standard Bank's SimplyBLU (POS, inventory, e-commerce and reporting) is the only African case with a retention-type result: merchant attrition down 4% in 2025, new business up 24%. Nedbank's SimplyBiz is a free, bank-agnostic support community with more than 164,000 members, but it reports no conversion to Nedbank clients.

### Cited Findings

**Case 12: FNB / FirstRand (South Africa). Built: free Instant Accounting, Instant Payroll and Instant Invoicing for business-account holders**
- Pricing model: "FREE to business account clients". This covers free set-up and support and free access to historical data, and accountants or bookkeepers can become providers after free training. Instant Payroll is free and supports payslips and SARS filings (EasyFile integration for EMP501). Historical IRP5 data from other systems cannot be imported. [Marketing, snippet-only: fnb.co.za Instant Accounting and Instant Payroll pages](https://www.online.fnb.co.za/business-banking/instant-solutions/instant-payroll.html)
- Scale context: about 1.3m SME clients and 34% overall SME market share (largest in South Africa); 51% share in asset finance and 42% in revolving credit facilities; commercial segment +6% year on year to June 2025. The write-up does **not** mention non-lending tools. [Award write-up] [Global Finance, 13 Nov 2025](https://gfmag.com/award/award-winners/worlds-best-sme-banks-2026-africa/)
- FY to June 2026, "community economy" segment:
  - More than 250,000 active business customers.
  - SME advances +31%.
  - Segment revenue +14% to R4.2bn; deposits +8%.
  - Group-level: 8.16m digitally active customers; FNB Connect processed R26bn.

  No figures on accounting, payroll or invoicing tools. [Reported via press] [BusinessDay, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-09-16-fnb-ceo-bullish-as-deposits-top-r1-trillion-and-private-banking-nears-2-million-clients)

**Case 13: Standard Bank (South Africa). SimplyBLU merchant operating platform (POS, inventory, e-commerce, reporting)**
- Upgrade on 7 Nov 2024:
  - Android card machine accepting card, QR and cash, with a barcode scanner.
  - Real-time inventory across in-store and online sales.
  - Integrated online store.
  - Merchant app with daily, weekly and monthly reports and expense tracking.
  - Reporting and insights at "no extra cost". No fees or merchant counts were disclosed.

  [Reported] [Standard Bank newsroom, 7 Nov 2024](https://www.standardbank.co.za/southafrica/news-and-media/newsroom/new-and-improved-simplyblu-the-ultimate-all-in-one-solution-for-sme-payments-and-business-management)
- **2025 results:**
  - New business on SimplyBLU +24%.
  - Turnover from new business +19%.
  - **Merchant attrition down 4%**.
  - Sales made by POS, QR, payment link or online store feed shared records for settlement, reconciliation, inventory and reporting.

  [Award write-up] [Euromoney, 17 Jul 2026](https://www.euromoney.com/article/cmserdmp5fk0g8k44g8co4wcw/corporate-banking/africas-best-sme-banking-ecosystem-2026-standard-bank/)
- Context:
  - December 2024: transacting merchant base +8% year on year, digital transaction volumes +15%. [Reported via press] [Africa.com, 4 Feb 2025](https://africa.com/media/businesses-cash-in-on-sas-booming-card-payments-and-e-commerce-revolution)
  - 2025 festive season: digital transactions +10.5% to 74.15m, worth R35.62bn; e-commerce volumes +40%. [Reported via press] [Bizcommunity, 30 Jan 2026](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/standard-bank-sees-double-digit-digital-transaction-growth-amid-40-rise-in-e-commerce-volumes-951974a)
- SimplyBLU launched in June 2019 and is "powered by Visa" (Mastercard case study also cited). [Snippet-only. The Mastercard PDF returned 403; the "powered by Visa" label comes from Bizcommunity and Africa.com]

**Case 14: Nedbank (South Africa). Sponsored: SimplyBiz, a free, open SME support community (non-clients included)**
- More than 164,000 members as of April 2026; free sign-up; nearly 50 SME initiatives in 2025. Features include:
  - A 400-term business glossary in 12 languages.
  - Coaching programmes (Flowcode, Silver SLAP).
  - Speed-networking zones.
  - Market stall and event sponsorships.

  It won Digital Banker's Best SME Beyond Banking Services Initiative and was named a G20 SME best-practice case. No conversion, revenue or deposit data. [Sponsored content] [BusinessTech (presented by Nedbank), 31 Jul 2026](https://businesstech.co.za/news/industry-news/868303/international-recognition-for-south-africas-smme-support-platform-simplybiz-powered-by-nedbank/)
- Earlier member counts of 132,000 and 150,000 appear in Nedbank G20 and SimplyBiz pages. [Snippet-only]

### Inferences
- South African banks give the tools away free to drive transactional primacy. None discloses adoption or the economics of tool users vs non-users, which suggests the metrics are either weak or commercially sensitive.
- Standard Bank's attrition figure is the only quantified retention benefit linked to an operational platform in Africa. Without a baseline, "down 4%" is ambiguous (relative or percentage points).

### Gaps
- No verified data for Absa (Business Evolve, Absa Access), Capitec Business, Ecobank (Omni Lite, SME Club), Equity Bank (EazzyBiz, Equitel) or KCB. These were not reached before the search budget ran out.

## Q7. Failures, exits and shutdowns

### Takeaway
Few outright shutdowns could be verified. The clearest bank-built non-lending SME venture that was exited is Standard Chartered / SC Ventures' Solv India, a B2B marketplace and supply-chain platform for MSMEs. It was loss-making and sold to Jumbotail in June 2025, with SC booking a gain of about US$0.2bn. Other changes are absorptions or re-platformings (Mashreq NEOBIZ into Mashreq Biz; CBA's Albert tablet replaced by Smart terminals), not failures.

### Cited Findings

**Case 15: Standard Chartered / SC Ventures, Solv India (B2B e-commerce and MSME supply-chain platform). Incubated 2019, sold 2025**
- FY24 (Inc42's sources): operating revenue INR132 Cr (about $15m, +57%); loss INR375 Cr (about $43m, +40%). Total funding was about $130m, about 91% from SC Ventures; the last valuation was about $200m. Reported reasons for the sale:
  - Losses weighing on Standard Chartered's P&L.
  - Asset offloading since 2023.
  - A wish to deconsolidate Solv.

  The IPO plan was off track, and CEO Amit Bansal was terminated before the deal. The deal was reportedly worth $40–50m in cash and equity, with SC Ventures taking about 30% of Jumbotail. [Press, anonymous sources] [Inc42, 22 Apr 2025](https://inc42.com/features/jumbotail-solv-deal-zero-profits-2x-valuation-jump-questions-galore/)
- Completed 30 June 2025 after Competition Commission of India approval. SC expected a gain of about $0.2bn in its Q2 2025 results; the combined platform serves more than 500,000 small retailers in 400 cities. [Reported] [Standard Chartered press release, 30 Jun 2025](https://www.sc.com/en/press-release/sc-ventures-incubated-solv-india-acquired-by-jumbotail/)

**Other exits and re-platformings (lower confidence)**
- Mashreq NEOBIZ: customers were migrated into Mashreq Biz from Feb 2025. This is an absorption, not a closure. [Mashreq, 24 Feb 2025](https://www.mashreq.com/en/uae/news/2025/february/mashreq-launches-mashreq-biz/)
- CBA: the Albert tablet was succeeded by the Smart terminal. [Snippet-only (Inside Retail)]

### Inferences
- Solv shows the risk of a bank building a full non-bank operating business (marketplace or commerce) rather than embedding tools in banking. It was cash-hungry (about $43m annual loss on $15m revenue) and was sold, even though the accounting outcome was a gain. Bank-adjacent ventures tend to be exited when they weigh on group P&L, regardless of strategic logic.

### Gaps
- I could not verify other suspected shutdowns or retirements (e.g., any UOB BizSmart partner removals, the status of FNB Instant Accounting, Emirates NBD E20 brand status, ANZ GoBiz) or any Asian bank SME super-app closures.

## Q8. Cross-cutting: is there evidence that SME clients using these tools have higher balances, income, retention, product holding or better credit outcomes?

### Takeaway
Hard, SME-specific, user-vs-non-user evidence is scarce in APAC and MEA. The available signals are:
- **DBS (2017):** digital consumer and SME customers earned a 27% ROE against 19%, with roughly 2x revenue per customer. The data is old and mixed with consumers.
- **UOB Malaysia:** almost a third of SmartBusiness adopters made UOB their main bank.
- **Standard Bank SimplyBLU:** merchant attrition down 4% in 2025.
- **Wio:** a profitable SME-first stack with NPS 75, though profit cannot be attributed to the tools.
- **CBA:** non-lending benefits deliberately gated by transaction volume and lending relationship.

No bank in the sample published credit-performance differences for tool users.

### Cited Findings
- DBS: 27% ROE for digital consumer and SME customers vs 19% for traditional; digital customers grew at a 23% CAGR vs -2%. [Euromoney, 20 Dec 2017](https://www.euromoney.com/article/b163mv0dk41x45/dbs-top-of-the-digital-class). In Singapore and Hong Kong, the 42% of customers who were digital produced 63% of profit, with S$1,300 vs S$600 revenue per customer (2017). [Jakarta Post, 12 Jul 2018](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2018/07/12/dbs-starts-to-enjoy-returns-from-digitalization.html)
- UOB Malaysia: "almost a third of SMEs that adopted UOB SmartBusiness switched to UOB Malaysia as their main banking partner" (undated result, published Jan 2024). [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/uob-smartbusiness-propels-sme-towards-digital-efficiency-and-growth)
- UOB-cited study: SMEs that adopted digital initiatives in 2020 saw stronger revenue growth than non-adopters (UOB SME Outlook 2021). [Xero release, 26 Jul 2021](https://www.xero.com/us/media-releases/xero-teams-up-with-uob)
- Standard Bank: SimplyBLU merchant attrition down 4% in 2025, new business +24%, turnover from new business +19%. [Euromoney, 17 Jul 2026](https://www.euromoney.com/article/cmserdmp5fk0g8k44g8co4wcw/corporate-banking/africas-best-sme-banking-ecosystem-2026-standard-bank/)
- Wio: NPS 75 for Wio Business; net profit AED622m in 2025; 120,000 business entities. [Entrepreneur ME, 18 Mar 2026](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/business-news/wio-bank-reports-aed-1-24-billion-revenue-up-55-in-2025)
- CBA: two Yello for Business benefit tiers assigned by transaction volumes and lending relationship, assessed monthly, across more than 340,000 eligible customers. [CBA, 18 Mar 2025](https://www.commbank.com.au/articles/newsroom/2025/03/yello-for-business-expansion.html)
- DBS: 20,000+ corporate and SME users of the DBS Joy assistant, with CSAT +23% (FY2025). [DBS AR 2025 via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/dbs-2025-annual-report-ce7e5fd9da8af723)
- HDFC / Mintoak: "activation rates, total payment throughput, and merchant engagement score" rose, but no figures were given. [PR Newswire, 5 Dec 2022](https://prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/mintoak-develops-merchant-solutions-to-fuel-business-growth-for-msmes-861588478.html)

### Inferences
- The pattern across regions is that banks disclose reach (users, members, eligible customers) and occasionally primacy or attrition, but almost never revenue, deposits or credit performance linked to tool use. For the report, "proven" results in APAC and MEA reduce to the five signals above.
- Pricing models observed:
  - Free and bundled with the account: FNB, Nedbank SimplyBiz (open to non-clients), BRI LinkUMKM, CBA Yello for Business.
  - Subsidised partner trial, then partner subscription: UOB with Xero (six months free).
  - Included in acquiring fees: Standard Bank SimplyBLU (reporting "at no extra cost") and CBA Smart Hospitality (pay only terminal rental and transaction fees; snippet-only).
  - White-label PaaS licence paid by the bank: HDFC and Mintoak.
- Delivery models observed:
  - Built: DBS, BRI, FNB, Wio, Mashreq, Emirates NBD.
  - Partnered or curated: UOB, CBA (Doshii, Scrypt), HDFC (Mintoak, Vyaparify).
  - Joint venture: SMBC and Money Forward.
  - Incubated and divested: SC / Solv.

### Gaps
- No SME-only, user-vs-non-user comparison of balances, income, product holding or credit losses was found for any bank in the region for 2023–2026.
- The DBS SME-specific digital ROE and income split (if DBS published it in its 2018–2020 annual reports) could not be verified.
